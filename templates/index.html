Supervised Learning Architecture & Formulation

The recommender uses a strictly supervised machine learning formulation to predict collaboration probabilities: $\hat{y} = P(Y = 1 \mid \mathbf{x})$. Training is conducted strictly on labeled student-faculty pairs where positive labels indicate confirmed co-authorship.
